# MetaWingman support

MetaWingman is research software and a Codex skill, not clinical, legal, regulatory, or publication approval. Support covers reproducible installation, schema validation, deterministic tools, documented workflow behavior, and confirmed defects in repository code.

When reporting a defect, include:

- MetaWingman release or commit;
- operating system, Python and R versions;
- the exact command and sanitized error output;
- the affected review stage and profile;
- a minimal redistributable fixture, when possible.

Do not attach credentials, cookies, institutional exports, copyrighted full text, identifiable participant data, confidential peer-review material, or a live private review project to a public report. Reduce the case to synthetic data or use a private channel supplied by the project maintainers.

Scientific-method disputes, database subscription procurement, account recovery, model-provider billing, journal submission, and adjudication of review decisions remain the user's or institution's responsibility.
