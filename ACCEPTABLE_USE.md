# Acceptable use

Use MetaWingman only with lawful access to data, full text, databases, model providers, and computing resources.

Do not use it to:

- bypass paywalls, CAPTCHAs, robots restrictions, access controls, licenses, or rate limits;
- share institutional credentials, cookies, tokens, or restricted database exports;
- fabricate searches, records, full text, screening decisions, extracted values, analyses, references, RoB/GRADE ratings, or human review;
- treat AI output as an independent human decision when the selected method requires a person;
- make clinical decisions or claims without qualified human review and the necessary evidence;
- submit a protocol, registration, manuscript, review decision, or other external artifact without explicit authorization;
- expose identifiable participant, confidential peer-review, unpublished, or proprietary content to an unauthorized provider.

Users remain responsible for scientific validity, database and publisher terms, research ethics, privacy law, institutional policy, conflict declarations, final conclusions, and publication submissions.
