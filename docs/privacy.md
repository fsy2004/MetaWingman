# Privacy Policy

MetaWingman is a local, self-contained skill for LLM hosts. It runs on your
machine and your chosen model provider.

- **No data collection.** The skill does not run a server, does not collect
  analytics, and does not transmit data to any MetaWingman-operated service.
- **Where your data goes.** Your review documents, search exports, and
  analysis data stay in your own working directory. When you use a hosted
  model API (for example, your own OpenAI or DeepSeek account), the content
  you send follows that provider's terms and privacy policy.
- **What the skill stores locally.** Audit logs, execution receipts, and
  intermediate files under the project's `validation-output/` directory.
  No credentials are written into the repository or the skill.
- **License obligations.** Downloaded full texts and datasets keep their
  original licenses; the skill records each license for audit.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/fsy2004/MetaWingman/issues
