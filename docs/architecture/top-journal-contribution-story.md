## One Scientific Story

Current scientific agents show that a strong paper can be organized around an entire research loop rather than a collection of isolated tools. The AI Scientist packages conception-to-publication automation; Co-Scientist packages test-time search over scientific hypotheses; the Virtual Lab packages differentiated specialists, scientific tools, human direction, and external experimental validation; Robin packages literature, analysis, observation, and hypothesis revision as a continuous state. AI-Researcher adds a benchmark spanning guided and open-ended computational research. These systems do not validate unattended evidence synthesis, but their contribution structure is transferable.

MetaWingman's story is:

> Evidence synthesis begins before a protocol is written: the system must decide which synthesis is worth doing and then acquire enough evidence to support its conclusions. MetaWingman is a lifecycle-complete evidence-synthesis scientist that builds a time-bounded evidence landscape, selects decision-relevant and feasible review opportunities, and allocates acquisition and verification by their expected effect on conclusion risk while preserving typed lineage and accountable stopping.

This wording remains a design hypothesis until end-to-end evaluation. "Scientist" describes the integrated research workflow; it does not imply consciousness, authorship, autonomous accountability, or human equivalence.

## Packaging Pattern Learned From Primary Papers

The strongest full-workflow agent papers do not rely on workflow length alone. They combine four elements:

1. **a consequential closed loop:** an entire research or experimental responsibility chain, not an isolated benchmark task;
2. **one legible control principle:** tree search, tournament evolution, differentiated tool specialists, continuous experimental state, a shared world model, or simulation-to-real adaptation;
3. **an external reality anchor:** executed experiments, wet-laboratory validation, real instruments, held-out tasks, published expert work products, or prospectively observed outcomes;
4. **mechanism evidence and limits:** ablations, scaling curves, failure analysis, and an explicit domain boundary.

MetaWingman therefore uses lifecycle completeness to establish importance, topic opportunity control plus conclusion-directed evidence control to establish method novelty, and time-sealed topic rediscovery plus counterfactual protocol replay to establish diagnostic validity. Prospective topic registration and living-update evaluation supply the closest available external reality anchors. A fluent manuscript, an LLM judge score, or a successful synthetic fixture cannot substitute for these results.

## Four Contributions

### C1. Decision-aware topic opportunity control

A temporal multidisciplinary evidence landscape links primary research, reviews, protocols, registrations, guidelines, priorities, PICO/PECO concepts, results and uncertainty. Candidate questions are generated from graph paths and gaps, then filtered by decision relevance, unresolved uncertainty, feasibility, evidence maturity, nonduplication, update need, equity, cross-domain value, contamination and ambiguity. A diversity-aware portfolio prevents repeated paraphrases from masquerading as broad discovery.

Required evidence:

- time-sealed historical targets across multiple fields and venue strata;
- baselines for bibliometrics, semantic gap maps, LLM-only, graph-only and literature-idea agents;
- false-opportunity controls for saturated, infeasible, empty-evidence and false-novelty topics;
- model-memory audits and claim separation between historical concordance and independent discovery;
- prospectively registered candidates with fixed follow-up outcomes.

### C2. Lifecycle-complete system

One typed evidence state spans topic selection, protocol and registration, source-specific search and lawful retrieval, deduplication and screening, multimodal documents, report-study-result lineage, extraction, RoB and missing evidence, poolability, deterministic R synthesis, certainty and claims, AI review and revision, and living updates.

Required evidence:

- the machine-audited coverage matrix passes;
- each stage can emit and consume typed artifacts in an end-to-end run;
- the paper reports native, guarded, external-handoff, and unvalidated areas separately;
- at least two materially different review families complete historical reconstruction.

### C3. Conclusion-directed evidence control

The controller allocates search, retrieval, full-text, screening opposition, model strength, verifier calls, and test-time compute from criterion-level residual omission risk multiplied by downstream claim impact and asymmetric harm. It can return `continue`, `stop_candidate`, or `abstain`; it cannot finalize a production-review stopping decision.

Required evidence:

- compare against fixed Top-K, a linear protocol pipeline, statistical/SAFE-style stopping without claim impact, and confidence-only routing;
- freeze cost and safety budgets before held-out runs;
- report false exclusion, included-evidence recall, claim accuracy, selective coverage, and cost-quality frontiers;
- ablate criterion risk, downstream impact, source diversity, deterministic conflicts, and routing.

### C4. Published-expert and counterfactual protocol benchmark

Published reviews expose expert final answers but can contaminate model knowledge and contain reference errors. We first measure agreement with the published expert reference, using only verified corrected versions and excluding unresolved integrity cases. For the same sealed source corpus, change exactly one protocol criterion, outcome hierarchy, time bound, estimand, or decision threshold. The counterfactual target is the expected delta across selection, lineage, analysis, certainty, and claim artifacts. Intervene on the first discrepant event and replay to test causal responsibility.

Required evidence:

- preregister valid perturbations and expected changed/unchanged nodes;
- keep original answers, post-cutoff evidence, and perturbation targets sealed until run lock;
- compare final-output scoring, ordinary stage attribution, transcript failure labels, and causal replay;
- report localization accuracy, downstream recovery after intervention, propagation depth, and attribution stability.

## Why Breadth Is Defensible

General biomedical agents often define breadth as the number of tools, databases, domains, or benchmark tasks. MetaWingman uses a different unit: **coverage of the scientific responsibility chain**. A capability counts only when its inputs, outputs, evidence, authority, verifier, failure state, and downstream consequences are explicit.

Accordingly, breadth has three non-interchangeable axes:

1. lifecycle breadth: all ten review stages have state and gates;
2. methodology breadth: every declared review profile has an authority and synthesis route, including `not applicable` or external handoff;
3. validation breadth: real reconstruction and prospective evidence, reported independently from implementation.

The current auditable implementation is broad on the first axis, uneven on the second, and still pre-validation on the third. That is a development result, not a weakness to hide.

## Evaluation Figures Before Prose

The eventual paper story should be supportable by five figures:

1. temporal evidence landscape to topic portfolio to protocol-to-living-claim architecture;
2. historical Top-K topic rediscovery, false-opportunity controls and prospective candidate outcomes;
3. lifecycle/profile/validation coverage map with no binary "supports everything" coloring;
4. end-to-end stage-loss and causal propagation waterfall across held-out review families;
5. risk-coverage-cost frontiers and ablations for topic and conclusion-directed control.

If these figures cannot be produced from frozen experiments, the corresponding headline claim must be narrowed.

## Claim Ladder

- **Now:** implemented architecture, typed contracts, fixture-tested mechanisms, and explicit validation gaps.
- **After published reconstruction:** retrospective end-to-end performance on named review families under historical cutoffs.
- **After held-out ablation:** evidence that conclusion-directed control contributes beyond reused mechanisms.
- **After prospective validation:** calibrated performance within the observed profiles, providers, and update settings.

Never claim "first", "fully autonomous", "human-level", "superhuman", "comprehensive validation", "all review types", or labor savings without a direct, appropriately powered comparison. AI-first means that models execute the default reversible workflow; human-overseen means abstentions, disagreements, high-risk scientific judgments, credentials, irreversible actions, and final responsibility remain governed.
